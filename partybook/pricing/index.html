Pricing

One price. Per branch, per year, paid in advance.

PartyBook

₹999per branch, per year

Everything in the app, with no limit on the number of orders you take. No tiers to choose between and nothing held back.

₹999 is an introductory price for early customers. It will go up for new customers as the app grows — we would rather say so now than surprise you later. If it changes while you are a customer you get at least 30 days' notice by email, and it never applies to a year you have already paid for.

Per branch, not per hotel

A hotel with three branches pays for three, because each branch has its own bill book, its own menu and its own staff. A hotel with one pays for one. You can renew every branch in one payment.

What is not charged

  • No per-order fee and no commission. Charging per order would tax you for using the app more, which is the opposite of the point. We move none of your money, so we take none of it.
  • No limit on orders. Take five bookings a month or five hundred; the price is the same.
  • No GST. DevRocks is not registered for GST, so the price above is final and no tax is added.
  • No setup fee. Creating your hotel, your branches and your first administrator account is free.

Paying

Billing is prepaid and yearly. You pay by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay, and your subscription extends by itself once the payment goes through — there is nothing to email us about and nobody to wait for.

Renewing early never costs you the days you have already paid for: a renewal is added to the end of the year you are in, not to today.

A late payment does not lock you out mid-booking. There is a week of grace after your renewal date, and even past that you can still read and print existing orders and record payments people owe you — only new bookings stop.

Cancelling

Stop any time. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
