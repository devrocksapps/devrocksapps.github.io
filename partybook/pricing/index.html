Pricing

Per branch, per month, paid in advance.

Limited

₹99per branch, per month

For a single shop taking a handful of orders a week. A soft cap of around 50 orders a month.

Unlimited

₹199per branch, per month

No cap on orders. For a busy kitchen, or a branch that has outgrown Limited.

The cap is soft, and that is deliberate. Going past 50 orders on Limited does not stop you taking a booking. This app is how a shop takes an order, and blocking that mid-sale would be a worse failure than us losing a little revenue. You see an upgrade prompt in the app instead.

If a branch exceeds the cap for three months running, its next renewal offers Unlimited only.

Per branch, not per hotel

A hotel with three branches pays for three, because each branch has its own bill book, its own menu and its own staff. A hotel with one pays for one.

What is not charged

  • No per-order fee and no commission. Charging per order would tax you for using the app more, which is the opposite of the point. We move none of your money, so we take none of it.
  • No GST. DevRocks is not registered for GST, so the prices above are final and no tax is added.
  • No setup fee. Creating your hotel, your branches and your first administrator account is free.

Paying

Billing is prepaid and monthly, and renewal is manual — we send a payment request and you pay it, by UPI or a payment link. You choose the tier up front; we never bill you after the fact for a month you have already used.

A late payment does not lock you out mid-booking. There is a grace window before a branch's account is suspended.

Cancelling

Stop any time. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Check these figures before publishing — they come from the plan rather than from a live price list, and they are what a customer will hold you to.
