Echo privacy policy

The short version: Echo collects nothing about you, and nothing it keeps ever leaves your phone.

Last updated 12 October 2026

Echo is a puzzle game for Android made by DevRocks, a sole proprietorship in India. Questions about this page go to partybook.support@gmail.com.

1. What is collected

Nothing. Echo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for internet access, so it has no way to send anything from your phone to us or to anyone else.

2. What is kept on your phone

So that you don't lose your place, Echo saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

Progress
Which levels are unlocked, stars, best tick counts, achievements and your Daily Loop streak.
Settings
Music, sound, vibration and control preferences, and which tips you have already seen.
Levels you make
Your work in the level editor.

None of it identifies you, and none of it is sent anywhere. To remove it, use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall Echo, which deletes everything it stored.

3. Sharing a level

When you share a level from the editor, Echo copies a text code to your clipboard and you decide where to paste it. The code holds only the level's layout and a solution — nothing about you or your device.

4. Permissions

Echo asks for one permission, vibration, for short haptic feedback while you play. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

5. Children

Echo collects no information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

6. Grievances

Complaints and questions about privacy go to the Grievance Officer, Sarankumar R, DevRocks, at partybook.support@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days. See Contact.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Echo changes any of the above — for example by adding online leaderboards — this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
