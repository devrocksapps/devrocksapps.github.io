Pantry Pop

Sort the goods on the night-market stalls. Three of a kind in a row pop, and the goods behind them come forward.

How it plays

Drag goods from stall to stall, or tap one and tap where it goes. Line up three of the same kind on a shelf and they pop. Clear a row and the goods waiting behind it slide forward. Empty every stall before the clock runs out, and chain matches for combo coins.

300 levels, and more

From three small stalls to ten stalls four rows deep, with a harder level every tenth. Every level is checked to be winnable.

Decorate your stall

Spend the coins you win on awnings, lanterns, signs, plants and a cat for the counter. Every few decorations level up your stall.

Free, with ads

No account, no in-app purchases, and it plays offline. Videos for boosters and extra time are your choice. See the privacy policy.

Getting it

Pantry Pop is coming to Google Play for Android. It is free.

Help

Stuck? Hint shows a good next move, Undo takes one back and +Shelf gives you room. For anything else, write to devrocksapps@gmail.com.
