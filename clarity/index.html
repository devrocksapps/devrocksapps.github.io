Clarity

A water sort puzzle game that keeps its promises.

How it plays

Pour liquid between glasses until every glass holds one colour. You can only pour onto a matching colour or into an empty glass, and only as much as fits. Every level can be solved with the glasses you start with, and Retry gives you the same board again, not a reshuffled one.

223 levels, one new idea at a time

Odd-sized glasses, frozen caps, valves, sealed vaults, fusing dyes, five-deep tubes, prisms and burettes. Each one gets its own level to teach it before it is ever combined with another.

Daily Challenge and Unmix

A new puzzle every day, and Unmix: a sorted board you have to scramble into a target pattern. Neither ever asks you to watch an ad.

Built to be read

Every liquid has its own pattern as well as its colour, so the game works for colour-blind players. It plays offline and has no account. See the privacy policy for how ads work.

Getting it

Clarity is coming to Google Play for Android. It is free, with ads.

Help

Stuck? Undo, Retry and a hint are always there. For anything else, write to devrocksapps@gmail.com.
